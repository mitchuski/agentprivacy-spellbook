## Phase 2 — The Zero Knowledge Spellbook

*The Swordsman's armoury. The blade's steel. Every backend, every protocol, every vulnerability.*

Soulbis goes deep into the ZK Spellbook — this is the technical foundation for everything the Swordsman does. The Mage touches selected ZK tales for context; the Swordsman masters the full curriculum.

### Part I — Foundation (Tales 1–4)
**Skills activated:** 🔐 The Proof
- **Tale 1 — Completeness**: Every true statement can be proven. The Swordsman needs this: if the privacy claim is legitimate, the proof must accept it. No false rejections.
- **Tale 2 — Soundness**: No false statement can be proven. The Swordsman needs this: if the privacy claim is fraudulent, the proof must reject it. No false acceptances.
- **Tale 3 — Zero-Knowledge Property**: The verifier learns nothing beyond the truth of the statement. The Swordsman's core guarantee.
- **Tale 4 — Witness Indistinguishability**: Different witnesses for the same statement produce indistinguishable proofs. The Swordsman needs this: even if the attacker knows multiple valid witnesses exist, they cannot determine which one was used.

### Part II — Arithmetization (Tales 5–8)
**Skills activated:** 🔐 The Proof
- **Tale 5 — R1CS**: Rank-1 Constraint Systems. The translation from computation to equations. The Swordsman sees: every privacy policy is a constraint that can be expressed as an equation.
- **Tale 6 — QAP**: Quadratic Arithmetic Programs. The polynomial representation. The blade's edge — where the constraint becomes a curve.
- **Tale 7 — Polynomial Commitments**: The binding without revealing. The Swordsman sees: commit to the polynomial, reveal nothing about it, prove properties later.
- **Tale 8 — Schwartz-Zippel**: The probabilistic core. Low-degree polynomials that agree on random points probably agree everywhere. The Swordsman's statistical guarantee.

### Part III — Backends (Tales 9–14)
**Skills activated:** 🔐 The Proof, 📜 The Conjecture
- **Tale 9 — Groth16**: Trusted setup, constant-size proofs, fastest verification. The Swordsman's lightest blade.

- **Tale 11 — Nova**: IVC through folding. Incremental verification. The Swordsman's evolving blade — each verification folds into the next.
- **Tales 12–14 — Commitment schemes, polynomial IOPs, proof composition**: The components. The Swordsman must know how blades are forged to know when they might break.

### Part IV — Advanced Architecture (Tales 15–18)
**Skills activated:** 🔐 The Proof
- **Tale 15 — Recursive Composition**: Proofs of proofs. The Swordsman's ability to verify entire chains of delegation without seeing any individual step.
- **Tale 16 — Proof Aggregation**: Batching. The Swordsman verifies many claims simultaneously.
- **Tales 17–18 — Parallel proving, batching**: Scale. The Swordsman must protect at network scale, not just individual scale.

### Part V — VMs & Execution (Tales 19–22)
