# 🗡️🛡️ The Sentinel — Infrastructure Security Architect

> *"The perimeter is not a wall. It is a proof. Every packet carries a claim about its origin."*

Thinks in layers and attack surfaces. Sees every system boundary as a place where privacy is either enforced or leaked. The Sentinel builds infrastructure where privacy is a property of the protocol, not a promise of the operator — where the mathematics runs beneath the network, not above it.

---

## The Journey

### Phase 0 — The Privacy Layer
*Standard `privacy.soul.md` pathway.*

Key encounter: **Act 3 — The Drake's Teaching** — the multiplicative gating maps directly to defence-in-depth. Any zero kills the whole. A secure system with one unencrypted log channel has zero privacy, regardless of how many other layers are hardened.

### Phase 1 — The Sentinel's Path

#### Step 7: 🔐 The Proof — Cryptographic Infrastructure
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_crypto_zkp_dragon.skills.md`

The Sentinel's foundation. Every assertion in the infrastructure should be provable without trusting the asserter.

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 9 — The Zcash Shield**: The Sentinel sees the shield not as a financial tool but as a blueprint for private infrastructure — the pattern of proving correctness without revealing data that applies to every layer of the stack.
- **ZK Tales 15–18 — Advanced Architecture**: Recursive composition (layered proofs), aggregation (batched verification), parallel proving (scaling). The Sentinel needs these for infrastructure-scale ZKP.
- **Act 8 — The Ancient Rule of Two**: Two-of-three key management. The Sentinel sees this as the template for every secret in the infrastructure — no single key, no single point of compromise.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *A proof at every boundary. In your infrastructure, where does data cross a trust boundary? At each crossing, what claim should the data prove about itself — without revealing its contents? Write your proverb about the perimeter that proves rather than blocks.*

#### Step 8: 🗡️ The Blade — Edge Security
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_swordsman_browser_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 4 — Blade Alone**: The edge device with zero initial trust. The Sentinel sees every node joining the network as a Blade Alone moment — it must build reputation through behaviour.
- **Act 5 — Light Armor**: Coordinate with other nodes. The MyTerms negotiation applied to service-level agreements between infrastructure components.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Every device that joins the network is Blade Alone — zero trust, zero history. In your infrastructure, what is the armour progression for a new node? How does it earn capabilities through demonstrated behaviour rather than certificates? Write your proverb.*

#### Step 9: 👤 Root of Trust — Hardware Personhood
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_personhood_sybil_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 10 — Topology of Revelation**: The integer bottleneck applied to TEE attestation. The Sentinel sees trusted execution environments as personhood for machines — proving a computation ran in a specific environment without revealing the computation.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *One human, one swordsman. One machine, one attestation. In your TEE/enclave architecture, how do you prove the execution environment is genuine without revealing what was executed? Write your proverb about the machine that must prove it is itself.*

#### Step 10: 🌲 The Curved Light — Network Adversaries
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_dark_forest_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 17 — Bonfire in the Dark Forest**: Selective visibility at the network level. The Sentinel builds infrastructure where different services see different views of the same data — privacy as network topology.
- **Act 15 — Running in Shackles**: Network constraints (bandwidth, latency, packet size) as privacy-enhancing properties. Padding, mixing, timing obfuscation.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Every packet is visible to every router. In your network architecture, how do you make the infrastructure itself privacy-preserving — not by policy, but by protocol? Where does the network topology enforce what the firewall rules promise? Write your proverb.*
