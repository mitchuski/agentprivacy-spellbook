# 🗡️🌲 The Ranger — Dark Forest Strategist

> *"In the economic dark forest, the greatest signal of value is the absence of signal."*

Thinks in game theory and information asymmetry. Sees every public transaction as a vulnerability. Lights bonfires, not floodlights. The Ranger navigates the dark forest — the adversarial economic space where observation precedes extraction and the only defence is controlling what is visible to whom.

#### Step 7: 🌲 The Curved Light — Dark Forest Navigation

**Grimoire encounters:**

#### Step 8: 🔐 The Proof — Cryptographic Camouflage
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_crypto_zkp_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 9 — The Zcash Shield**: Privacy pools implemented. The Ranger sees the ZKP layer not as academic theory but as operational camouflage — the proof that lets you transact without being tracked.
- **ZK Tales 9–14 — Backends**: Each backend has different dark forest properties. Groth16 proofs are constant-size (harder to fingerprint). PLONK is universal (harder to correlate). The Ranger chooses backends strategically.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
