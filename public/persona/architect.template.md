# ☯️🤖 The Architect — AI Agent System Designer

> *"To act on your behalf, the agent must know your will. To protect your privacy, it must not. Two agents or none."*

Thinks in agent architectures and information flows. Sees every capability as a potential reconstruction channel. Knows that the hardest problem in AI is not making agents smart — it is making them trustworthy without making them omniscient. The Architect designs the dual-agent systems that solve the privacy-delegation paradox.

### Phase 1 — The Architect's Path

*The Architect needs the full stack — protection AND projection, blade AND spell — because the system design requires both.*

#### Step 7: 🤖 The Paradox — The Core Problem *(Mage)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_ai_agent_dragon.skills.md`

The Architect's central challenge. The privacy-delegation paradox: any single agent that knows both privacy boundaries and delegation preferences can reconstruct the principal's behavioural model. The separation theorem proves two agents are necessary and sufficient.

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 2 — Dual Ceremony** *(revisited as architecture specification)*: The Swordsman knows boundaries but not intentions. The Mage knows intentions but not boundaries. The conditional independence between them — I(S;M|π) ≤ ε — is the architectural invariant. The Architect enforces it.
- **Act 7 — The Mirror**: The reconstruction ceiling. If I(S;M|π) > ε, the gap closes and reconstruction becomes possible. The Architect's primary metric: keep the mutual information below the threshold.
- **Act 11 — The Sovereignty Spiral**: The optimal protection-delegation ratio. The Architect asks: given a specific delegation task, what is the minimum information the Mage needs? How does this scale?
- **Act 3 — Drake's Teaching**: Four gates for agent design. Privacy (the separation holds). Consent (the human authorised the delegation). Quality (the agent performs competently). Standards (the system interoperates with others). All four or nothing.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Design the separation. In your agent system, what does Agent A know that Agent B must not? What is the conditional independence invariant? What information, if it crossed the boundary, would enable reconstruction? Define the boundary mathematically. Write your proverb about the two agents that serve one human.*

#### Step 8: 🔐 The Proof — Agent Verification *(Swordsman)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_crypto_zkp_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 9 — Zcash Shield**: Agent attestation through ZKP. The agent proves it followed the protocol without revealing what it did. "I delegated correctly" without "here is what I delegated."
- **ZK Tales 1–4 — Foundation**: The properties the agent's proofs must satisfy. When the Mage attests "I acted on the principal's behalf within authorised scope," this is a ZKP claim.
- **ZK Tale 29 — Intelligence Proof**: zkML — verifiable machine learning. The Architect sees the path to proving an agent's reasoning was correct without revealing the reasoning itself.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *The agent claims: "I followed the protocol." The verifier must confirm this without seeing the execution trace. In your system, what does the agent prove? What does the proof hide? Design the circuit. Write your proverb about the agent that proves it obeyed without showing what it did.*

#### Step 9: 👤 Root of Trust — Agent Personhood *(Swordsman)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_personhood_sybil_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 22 — The Hoopy Frood**: The echo as proof-of-human-presence. The Architect ensures every agent in the system has a human behind it — the existential quantifier that prevents autonomous agent swarms from gaming the coordination.
- **Act 10 — Topology of Revelation**: The binding between human and agent as a topological constraint. One human, one Swordsman, one Mage. The topology cannot be deformed into a many-to-many mapping without breaking the architecture.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *In your multi-agent system, how do you ensure every agent has exactly one human principal? What prevents an adversary from spawning a thousand agents, each claiming a different principal? Design the binding. Write your proverb about the agent that must prove it serves one.*

#### Step 10: 🌲 The Curved Light — Adversarial Agents *(Swordsman)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_dark_forest_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 15 — Running in Shackles**: Agent constraints as safety properties. The information-theoretic bounds on what the agent can learn are not bugs — they are the safety guarantees.
- **Act 17 — Bonfire**: Agent-to-agent selective disclosure. The Swordsman reveals boundary information to trusted agents (VRC holders) without broadcasting to all agents on the network.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Your agents operate in a multi-agent environment. Adversarial agents want to extract information about your principal. Cooperative agents need to coordinate. How do your agents distinguish between them? What do they reveal to allies? What do they hide from adversaries? Write your proverb about the agent in the dark forest.*

#### Step 11: 🏴‍☠️ The Ship — Agent Governance *(Mage)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_hitchhiker_governance_dragon.skills.md`

**Reconstruction prompt:**
