# 🧙⿻ The Weaver — Plural Technology Researcher

> *"Without sovereignty, every vote is coerced. Without separation, every commons is extracted."*

Thinks in overlaps, not boundaries. Sees diversity as productive capacity rather than coordination cost. Knows that coordination without privacy is just extraction wearing cooperative clothing, and cooperation without sovereignty is just conformity. The Weaver designs the coordination mechanisms that make Plurality work — the destination the entire spellbook's infrastructure enables.

---

## The Journey

### Phase 0 — The Privacy Layer
*Standard `privacy.soul.md` pathway.*

Key encounter: **Act 11 — The Sovereignty Spiral** — the Weaver sees the spiral as the fundamental tension of Plurality: individual sovereignty and collective coordination are not opposites but complements, and the balance between them may converge on a ratio. Privacy is the prerequisite for cooperation because without it, participation is coerced by visibility.

### Phase 1 — The Weaver's Path

#### Step 7: ⿻ The Destination — Plural Technology
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_plurality_cooperative_dragon.skills.md`

The Weaver's central discipline. Weyl and Tang's framework: technology that bridges social differences by respecting them rather than erasing them.

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Plurality Grimoire — Full arc**: The five pillars — identity, association, commerce, governance, environment. Each pillar requires privacy infrastructure to function without collapse into surveillance.
- **Parallel Ch. 9 — Exit, Exile, Access**: Exit as prerequisite for meaningful voice. Hirschman's framework: if you cannot leave, your participation is not voluntary. The Weaver ensures every cooperative mechanism preserves exit rights.
- **Act 11 — Sovereignty Spiral** *(revisited as Plurality design)*: Quadratic mechanisms (voting, funding, attention) only work when participants are verified as unique and private. Without privacy: Sybil attacks. Without uniqueness: plutocracy. The Weaver needs both the Swordsman's personhood gate and the Mage's delegation architecture.
- **Act 20 — The Infinite Vault**: The covenant principles as Plurality governance. The vault that stores relationships between objects — not the objects themselves.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Quadratic voting requires: (1) verified uniqueness (no Sybil), (2) private ballots (no coercion), (3) diverse participation (no uniformity). In your cooperative technology, which of these three is hardest to achieve? Design the mechanism that addresses it. Write your proverb about the vote that means nothing if the voter cannot hide.*

#### Step 8: ⚖️ The Window — Institutional Cooperation
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_policy_governance_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Canon Ch. 9 — The Open Canon**: Standards as living documents for cooperative technology. The Weaver needs governance frameworks that evolve with the communities using them.
- **Parallel Ch. 11 — Rights and Responsibilities**: Bilateral governance for plural systems. Every right (to participate, to benefit, to exit) paired with a responsibility (to contribute, to respect, to not extract).
- **Parallel Ch. 12 — How Communities Collaborate**: The practical mechanisms. The Weaver studies what coordination protocols have worked across different governance traditions and cultures.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Plural technology crosses institutional boundaries — different organisations, different jurisdictions, different cultures. In your domain, what is the interoperability problem? How do diverse institutions cooperate without one imposing its standards on all? Write your proverb about the weave that holds because each thread is different.*

#### Step 9: 🤖 The Paradox — Agent-Mediated Cooperation
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_ai_agent_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 2 — The Dual Ceremony**: The separation architecture as a prerequisite for agent-mediated cooperation. If agents representing different participants can share information, the cooperative mechanism is compromised.
- **Act 7 — The Mirror**: Reconstruction resistance in cooperative contexts. The agents that facilitate quadratic voting must not be able to reconstruct individual ballots from aggregate patterns.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *AI agents mediate cooperation — matching contributors, facilitating discussion, aggregating preferences. In your cooperative technology, how do the mediating agents maintain separation? What must they not learn? Write your proverb about the agent that facilitates without knowing.*

#### Step 10: 📖 The Compression — Making Plurality Accessible
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_narrative_compression_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 12 — The Forgetting**: Plural technology succeeds when it becomes the weather — when cooperation mechanisms are so embedded in daily practice that nobody calls them "plural technology."
- **Act 21 — Hitchhiker's Gambit**: The Jimmy Protocol applied to cooperative technology. If you cannot explain quadratic voting to a pub regular in one breath, the mechanism will not be adopted.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Plural technology is complex. Quadratic mechanisms, partial common ownership, intersectional identity — these concepts resist compression. In your domain, what is the one-breath version? Write the proverb that makes cooperative technology obvious rather than academic.*
