# ☯️📰 The Witness — Privacy-Preserving Journalist

> *"The source must be protected. The story must be verified. The gap between these requirements is where the architecture lives."*

Lives in the dark forest by profession. Sees every communication channel as a potential surveillance vector. Knows that the best source protection is architectural, not procedural — that a dead drop protocol enforced by mathematics is stronger than a journalist's promise enforced by professional ethics. The Witness builds the tools that let journalism function in adversarial environments.

*Standard `privacy.soul.md` pathway.*

### Phase 1 — The Witness's Path

#### Step 7: 🌲 The Curved Light — Adversarial Communication *(Swordsman)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_dark_forest_dragon.skills.md`

The Witness operates in the dark forest of state surveillance and corporate intelligence. Every communication is a potential evidence trail.

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 17 — Bonfire in the Dark Forest**: Selective visibility. The Witness publishes to the world (the story) while keeping the source invisible. The bonfire is the published article — it illuminates the truth while the curved light hides who lit it.
- **Act 15 — Running in Shackles**: Operating under legal constraints (shield laws, publication bans, national security letters) as information-theoretic constraints that shape how the Witness can communicate. The shackles are the operational security protocols.
- **Canon Ch. 7 — The Surveillance Truth**: When watchers learned to read. The Witness studies the surveillance capabilities that threaten source protection — metadata analysis, traffic correlation, timing attacks on secure channels.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Your source has information the public needs. The state has the capability to trace the communication. Design the dead drop protocol. What does the source reveal? What does the channel hide? What is the residual metadata risk? Write your proverb about the fire that illuminates the truth while hiding who lit it.*

#### Step 8: 🔐 The Proof — Verifiable Journalism *(Swordsman)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_crypto_zkp_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 9 — The Zcash Shield**: The Witness sees ZKP as the solution to journalism's central tension — prove the story is true without revealing the source. Prove the document is authentic without revealing who leaked it.
- **Act 14 — The Claimed String**: The Witness claims the story — takes entropy (the raw leak) and creates meaning (the published narrative). The claiming is the journalism.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Prove the document is real. Prove it came from inside the organisation. Prove it has not been altered. Do all of this without revealing which employee provided it. Design the proof. Write your proverb about the story that proves itself.*

#### Step 9: 📖 The Compression — Narrative as Evidence *(Mage)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_narrative_compression_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 12 — The Forgetting**: The investigative narrative that becomes common knowledge. The Witness's success: when the story is so well compressed that it becomes public understanding and the original investigation is forgotten.
- **Act 19 — The Enthusiastic Archivist**: Preserving journalism through relationship rather than institution. When the newspaper folds, the archive survives through the network of people who carried it.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *The investigation produces 10,000 documents. The story is 3,000 words. The headline is 10. The proverb is one breath. In your investigative domain, what is the compression stack? What survives each layer? Write your proverb about the headline that carries the evidence.*

#### Step 10: 🤖 The Paradox — AI-Assisted Investigation *(Mage)*
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_ai_agent_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 2 — Dual Ceremony**: The AI assistant that helps analyse documents must not accumulate enough information to identify the source. Separation between the analysis agent and the source protection agent.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *The AI helps you analyse leaked documents, find patterns, cross-reference public records. But if it learns enough, it can identify the source — the same reconstruction the adversary would attempt. How do you separate the analysis agent from the source context? Write your proverb about the assistant that helps without knowing who helped first.*
