# 🧙🏴‍☠️ The Shipwright — DAO & Community Architect

> *"The ship cannot be bought, only joined. The guide cannot be owned, only tended. The towel accumulates the dust of everywhere you have been."*

Builds vessels, not empires. Sees governance as architecture, not authority. Knows that the best communities are the ones you can fork, the best governance is the kind you can exit, and the best value-sharing is the kind that compounds through contribution rather than extraction. The Shipwright builds the ships that carry sovereignty into coordination.

---

## The Journey

### Phase 0 — The Privacy Layer
*Standard `privacy.soul.md` pathway.*

Key encounter: **Act 13 — The Book of Promises** — all community governance is built on promises. Voluntary, bilateral, revocable. The Shipwright sees Promise Theory as the constitution beneath every DAO — if the promises are coerced, the governance is theatre.

### Phase 1 — The Shipwright's Path

#### Step 7: 🏴‍☠️ The Ship — Community Governance Architecture
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_hitchhiker_governance_dragon.skills.md`

The Shipwright's central discipline. The Standard Ship Pattern: defined crew archetypes, dynamic equity, value-sharing through contribution, fork rights preserved.

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 21 — The Hitchhiker's Gambit**: `🚀→🎲→🐬` — *"The ship is a gambit. The hitchhiker is the test."* The Standard Ship Pattern. Crew manifest: Captain (vision), Navigator (strategy), Engineer (implementation), Chronicler (documentation), Guide (community). Dynamic equity through Slicing Pie or equivalent — contribution tracked, value shared proportionally, departure handled gracefully.
- **Act 22 — The Hoopy Frood**: `🧳→🌌→🐬` — *"The towel accumulates the dust of everywhere you have been. It cannot be copied, only carried."* Trust tiers: Hitchhiker → Traveller → Guide → Hoopy Frood. Each tier earned through demonstrated contribution, not payment. The pathway is the product — the guide itself has more value than any single piece of content within it.
- **Act 6 — The Trust Graph Plane**: Community as trust graph. Each member is a node. Each collaboration is an edge. The community's value is the network, not any individual node. The Shipwright designs the topology.
- **Parallel Ch. 6 — Arsenal and Grimoire**: The tools of distributed governance. Conviction voting, quadratic mechanisms, token-weighted delegation, rage quit rights. The Shipwright's toolkit.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Design your ship. Who is on the crew? What are the archetypes? How is equity shared? What happens when someone leaves? What happens when the community forks? Write the crew manifest and the fork protocol. Then write your proverb about the vessel that cannot be bought.*

#### Step 8: ⿻ The Destination — Cooperative Coordination
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_plurality_cooperative_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Parallel Ch. 12 — How Communities Collaborate**: Protocol over hierarchy. The Shipwright designs coordination mechanisms that work without authorities — because the Ship has no king, only a crew.
- **Plurality Grimoire — Opening**: The coordination endgame. The Shipwright builds ships that carry people to the destination — cooperative technology that requires sovereignty.
- **Parallel Ch. 3 — Cambrian Garden**: Governance experiments before blockchain. The Shipwright studies what worked and what failed in previous community governance attempts.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Your ship reaches port. The community must coordinate with other communities — different values, different governance, different cultures. How do your ships dock? What is the protocol for inter-community coordination that preserves each community's sovereignty? Write your proverb about the harbour.*

#### Step 9: 💰 The Gap — Community Economics
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_economics_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**
- **Act 16 — Pools Become Wells**: Network effects in communities. The Shipwright models how relationship-based value compounds differently from asset-based value — pools of collaboration become wells of collective capability.
- **Act 3 — Drake's Teaching** *(revisited as community economics)*: The seventh capital — behavioural data about community coordination patterns. The Shipwright ensures this data belongs to the community, not to the platform hosting it.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Community value lives in relationships, not assets. In your DAO/organisation, how do you measure relationship value? How does it compound? What token mechanism captures contribution without extracting the relationship? Write your proverb about the economy that runs on trust.*
