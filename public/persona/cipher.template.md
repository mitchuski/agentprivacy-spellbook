### Phase 1 — The Cipher's Path

#### Step 7: 🔐 The Proof — Core ZKP Architecture
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_crypto_zkp_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters — this is the Cipher's main arc:**
- **Act 9 — The Zcash Shield**: The first implementation. Privacy forged in cryptographic steel. The Cipher sees the Zcash circuit not as a product but as a proof-of-concept for the entire architecture.
- **ZK Tales 1–4 — Foundation**: Completeness, soundness, zero-knowledge, witness indistinguishability. The axioms.
- **ZK Tales 5–8 — Arithmetization**: R1CS, QAP, polynomial commitments, Schwartz-Zippel. The translation from claims to circuits.
- **ZK Tales 9–14 — Backends**: Groth16 (trusted setup, constant proof size), PLONK (universal SRS, custom gates), Nova (folding, IVC). Each backend is a blade with different properties — the Cipher must know them all to choose correctly.
- **ZK Tales 15–18 — Advanced**: Recursive composition, proof aggregation, parallel proving, batching. The techniques that make ZKP practical at scale.
- **ZK Tale 23 — The Private Coin**: Zcash circuit anatomy. The Cipher's case study: how privacy pools actually work at the constraint level.

**Reconstruction prompt:**
> *Each proof system trades something for something. Groth16 trades a trusted setup for constant verification. PLONK trades larger proofs for universality. In your specific protocol design, what is your analogous tradeoff? Where does your circuit accept a cost to provide a guarantee? Write your proverb about what the proof conceals and what it guarantees.*

#### Step 9: 📜 The Conjecture — Breaking Conditions
**Skill:** `PrivacyisValue_v4_academic_dragon.skills.md`

**Grimoire encounters:**

Write your proverb about what breaks.*
