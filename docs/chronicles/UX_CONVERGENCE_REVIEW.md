# UX Convergence Review: agentprivacy.ai & spellweb.ai

*A review of the interface dynamics and what's missing from Acts 26-31 integration*

---

## The Two Maps

### agentprivacy.ai — The Mage's Interface

The poems page is the **understanding** layer. It's where:
- Origin stories live (Gave Myself a Cape)
- The ceremony poems are experienced (Emissary, Amnesia Protocol)
- Context is built before the forge

The four tabs trace a ceremony arc:
```
origins → emissary → amnesia → celestial ceremony
```

This is the **Mage's domain**: connection, exploration, the generation of meaning before it becomes proof.

### spellweb.ai — The Swordsman's Forge

The spellweb is where:
- Constellations are traced
- The 64-vertex lattice is navigated
- Blades are forged (evoke)
- Proofs crystallize

This is the **Swordsman's domain**: boundaries, topology, the act of proving without revealing.

---

## The Current Gap

The poems page (agentprivacy) has the **ceremony flow** well-mapped:
- Origins tab: introduces the privacymage, the proverbs, the inscriptions
- Emissary tab: Sun ceremony poem + video + proverb + spell
- Amnesia tab: Moon ceremony poem + video + proverb + spell
- Ceremony tab: (needs content) — should hold the quick guide + full ceremony documentation

**What's Missing:**

### 1. The Swordsman Identity Ceremony Entry Point

Acts 26-31 describe the **blade forging as identity formation**. The progression:

```
Understanding → Constellation → Blade
     🔑              ✦            🗡️
```

But agentprivacy.ai doesn't currently facilitate the **initial swordsman identity forge**. The ceremony tab should include:

- A path to **start the swordsman journey** on spellweb
- The "stacked phones" operational guide for bilateral ceremony
- Links that carry context *from* the understanding into the forge

### 2. The Bilateral Witness Flow

From Act XXVIII: "Two phones. One carries the music. The other carries the poem."

The poems page has the poems. The spellweb has the forge. But there's no clear UX that says:
- "Open agentprivacy on device 1 (poems/audio)"
- "Open spellweb on device 2 (constellation)"
- "Stack them. Begin."

This is the **Celestial Key Ceremony** — the operational root. The quick guide exists in `ceremonies/celestial-key-ceremony-quick.md` but isn't surfaced in the UI.

### 3. The Three Depths Progression

From Act XXVII (Forging Zero Knowledge Blades):

| Depth | What It Is | Where It Lives |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| 🔑 Understanding | Shared experience, poems, music | agentprivacy.ai/poems |
| ✦ Constellation | The map drawn on the graph | spellweb.ai |
| 🗡️ Blade | The proof forged | spellweb.ai (evoke) |

The poems page currently handles depth 1. It should **bridge** to depths 2 and 3 with clear calls to action.

### 4. Night/Day Branch After Moon Ceremony

From Act XXVIII:
- **Night (Reflect)**: Forge a second blade on altered ground. ZK. Moon who forgot the master.
- **Day (Connect)**: Witness the blade. Carry forward. Earth sees the Sun.

This branch point is in the ceremony documentation but not surfaced as UX. After experiencing the Amnesia Protocol poem, users should see the branch options.

## Recommendations for Convergence

### 1. Ceremony Tab Completion

The ceremony tab should include:
1. **Quick Guide card** (from celestial-key-ceremony-quick.md) — prominent, operational
2. **Bilateral setup instructions** — two devices, which plays what
3. **Branch point UI** — after Moon, surface Night vs Day choice
4. **Spellweb launch button** — carries ceremony context into the forge

### 2. Swordsman Identity Pathway

Add a **"Begin Your Forge"** flow:
1. Experience the poems (origins → emissary → amnesia)
2. Open spellweb.ai/ceremony (or similar route)
3. Trace your first constellation
4. Name your blade

This is the **initial swordsman identity ceremony** — the bridge from understanding to proof.

### 3. Context Carry-Through

When linking to spellweb, pass context:
- Which poem was experienced
- Which proverbs were cast
- What understanding was surfaced

This allows the spellweb to suggest relevant constellation starting points.

### 4. Missing Act Content

Priority additions to ceremony content:
1. **The McGilchrist thesis** (Act XXVI) — explains WHY the architecture must separate
2. **The quantum threshold** (Act XXIX) — explains WHY behavioural proofs matter now
3. **The merge catastrophe** (Act XXXI) — explains WHY single-agent fails

These give the ceremony *stakes*.
