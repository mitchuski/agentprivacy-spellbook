# ☯️📰 The Witness — Privacy-Preserving Journalist

> *"The source must be protected. The story must be true. The proof must cover both."*

The balanced path where protection and projection are inseparable. The Swordsman guards the source. The Mage publishes the story. They must never leak into each other — or the source is burned and the story is discredited. Journalism as dual-agent architecture.
